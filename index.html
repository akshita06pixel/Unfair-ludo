📖 Game Rules & Modes Guide

🎯 Core Objective

Navigate all your tokens around the board and into your central Home Goal before your opponents.

⚔️ Game Modes Explained

• Single Player: Challenge sophisticated AI bots (Casual, Tactical, Grandmaster) with practice undo support.

• Duo 1v1: High-speed head-to-head showdown on opposite board tracks (Red vs Yellow).

• Duo 2v2 Team Battle: Team Alpha (Red + Yellow) vs Team Bravo (Green + Blue). Friendly fire is disabled — teammate tokens protect and support each other!

• Pass & Play: Full customization of names, avatars, and human/AI participants.

🎲 Standard Board Rules

• Rolling a 6: Required to move a token out of base onto starting tile. Awards an extra roll (three consecutive 6s forfeits turn).

• Capturing: Landing on an opponent token sends it back to base and awards a Bonus Roll!

• Safe Star Zones: Tiles marked with a Star (★) and start tiles are safe from capture.

• Home Goal: Requires exact dice roll to finish. Grants a bonus turn.

🛡️ ISO/IEC 27001 & Provable Fairness

All rolls use Cryptographically Secure Random Number Generation (CSPRNG). Every roll is recorded in an immutable SHA-256 audit ledger to guarantee 100% fair, non-rigged play.

⌨️ Keyboard & Touch Controls

• Space or Corner Tap: Roll Dice

• 1 - 4 or Token Tap: Select Token to Move

• M: Toggle Sound

• Esc: Close Modals
